FAQ

Pricing questions, answered straight.

Is the starting price what I'll actually pay?

If your needs match the standard scope, yes. If you need more — extra pages, locations, languages — the proposal shows the full figure before you commit, and it doesn't move after that.

How do payments work?

Projects: part upfront, balance at launch. Monthly plans: billed monthly, cancel anytime. We accept UPI, bank transfer and cards.

Can I upgrade later?

Yes, and you never pay twice for the same work. Start with Starter; if you move to Growth later, everything already built counts toward it.

What if I'm not happy?

Tell us early — revision rounds are built into every proposal. If we genuinely can't get it right, we'd rather part fairly than hold you to something that isn't working.
