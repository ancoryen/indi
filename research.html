How it works

Five steps from a hunch to a decision.

  1. 1

    Describe the decision

    Your idea, and the specific call you need to make. A quick AI check flags anything too vague before you spend a credit.

  2. 2

    Shape the audience

    We infer a target market from your idea. Adjust markets, ages, roles and how skeptical the panel should be.

  3. 3

    Generate the survey

    A short, structured questionnaire is written for your decision. Edit or regenerate any question.

  4. 4

    Run the panel

    Personas answer independently, with follow-up probes. Credits are spent only when you launch.

  5. 5

    Read the memo

    A verdict, the numbers behind it, the strongest objection, and the single next test worth running.

Fair pushback

A simulated panel isn't the truth. It's a faster rung on the ladder.

Ask biased friends and you'll hear what you want. Ask one AI and you'll get a confident average. Run real interviews and you'll wait weeks and pay for it. Research sits in between — fast and consistent enough to kill bad ideas early, honest enough to point you at the real test.

  • Friends & family Warm, biased, unreliable
  • One AI chat Fast, but a single agreeable voice
  • Indizilla Research A consistent panel that pushes back
  • Real interviews The truth — slow and expensive
  • Live experiment The final word, once you're committed

FAQ

The questions we get most.

Is this a replacement for real user research?

No — and we won't pretend otherwise. It's a fast, rigorous way to pressure-test an idea and find the sharpest questions before you invest in real interviews or a live test. Use it to decide what's worth testing for real.

How many personas answer each study?

A Quick Pulse study runs 50. Larger modes go up to 400 for higher-confidence reads across segments. You pick the mode when you launch, and it's shown in credits before you spend anything.

How much does a study cost?

You buy credits in packs and spend them per study — a Quick Pulse is 100 credits. There's no subscription. See the packs.

Can I dig into the results?

Yes. Every memo comes with the response mix and sample quotes, and you can ask the panel follow-up questions (2 credits each) to probe a segment or an objection further.

Who is this for?

Founders, product managers and marketers making an early call with incomplete information — the moment where a week of clarity is worth more than a month of debate.
