What the panel is telling you

On-campus students with unpredictable schedules are the clear core: they framed the box as “one less thing to think about,” and intent among that segment ran well above the panel average. The appetite for the idea is genuine — the concept isn't the problem.

The resistance is almost entirely about price. At ₹1,200/month, a large minority hesitated; the same people warmed up quickly when a lower entry tier was implied. Skeptics also questioned food quality and flexibility (“what if I skip a week?”), but those were objections to be answered, not dealbreakers.

Watch out for

Price sensitivity at ₹1,200. The idea is wanted; the current price is the thing most likely to sink it.

Recommended next test

Run a small Pulse study on two price points (₹799 vs ₹999) with the on-campus segment to find the ceiling before you commit to a city plan.

Reading this memo: a “conditional go” means the idea works for a specific segment under a specific condition — here, the right price. Confirm the condition with the next test before scaling.
