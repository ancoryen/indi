Salon · Chandigarh

Shine & Style: from invisible to fully booked

The problem
Fifteen years of loyal walk-ins, but zero online presence. New residents in the neighbourhood searched "salon near me" and found competitors two streets away.
Our approach
Visibility first, website second. We set up and optimized their Google Business Profile the first week, then built a one-page site with online booking so found customers could act immediately.
The solution
Optimized Google listing with fresh photos, a fast one-page website with a booking system, WhatsApp Business with quick replies, and a review request flow at the billing counter.
Lessons learned
The booking system mattered more than we expected — half the new bookings came after 9 pm, when the salon phone went unanswered. Visibility without a way to act loses the moment.

Restaurant · Pune

Patel's Kitchen: the Google listing became the storefront

The problem
Great food, weak first impression. An unclaimed Google listing showed wrong hours and three-year-old photos. Orders depended entirely on walk-past traffic.
Our approach
No website to start — the budget went where the customers were. We claimed and rebuilt the Google listing, designed a WhatsApp menu catalogue, and set up review management.
The solution
A fully optimized profile with a photographed menu, weekly Google posts, a shareable digital menu, and review responses within a day — every review, good or bad.
Lessons learned
Responding to old negative reviews mattered as much as collecting new ones. Customers told the owner they chose the restaurant because "even the complaints were answered politely."

Small manufacturer · Ludhiana

Sharma Precision Tools: B2B trust at first search

The problem
A 20-person workshop with excellent output and no website. Purchase managers who received their quotes searched the company name, found nothing, and moved on to suppliers who looked established.
Our approach
Build the trust signals a B2B buyer checks in the first five minutes: a professional website, a product catalogue with specifications, and email at their own domain.
The solution
A five-page website with a downloadable spec catalogue, machine and facility photography, quality certifications displayed up front, and business email for the whole sales team.
Lessons learned
In B2B, the website rarely generates leads directly — it stops the leads you already have from leaking away. Their conversion rate on sent quotes rose before traffic did.
