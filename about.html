About

This page isn't really about us. It's about you.

You run a good business. Your customers trust you once they meet you. The problem is everything that happens before they meet you — and that's the gap Indizilla exists to close.

Why we exist

Small businesses deserve serious work.

Somewhere along the way, small businesses got used to being handed the leftover version — template sites, recycled branding, work nobody stood behind. The size of the business became an excuse for the standard of the work.

Indizilla exists to close that gap: work built to the standard your customers judge you by, delivered without complexity, with everything owned by you.

"The barber who does great work deserves to look as good online as the chain salon with an agency retainer."
What we believe
The honest answer

Why are traditional agencies so expensive?

Not because they're dishonest. Because of what your invoice actually pays for:

Offices

Glass conference rooms in city-centre rents — added to every project bill.

Layers

Account managers who manage the people who manage the people who do the work.

Decks

Weeks of strategy presentations before anything gets built.

Minimums

Retainer floors set for corporate clients — sized to exclude you by design.

We removed all four. What's left is the work — and the work is what your customers actually see.

How we work

Small businesses deserve the same standards. Not smaller ones.

The same rigour a large brand gets, applied at a scale that fits — and spent only on things your customers will notice.

  • We say no to work that won't help you — even when it pays us.
  • We explain everything in plain English, then explain it again if needed.
  • We put prices on the website because you shouldn't have to ask.
  • We hand over every login, file and account. It's your business.
  • We'd rather keep you for years than upsell you today.
