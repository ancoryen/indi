FAQ

Questions we hear every week.

How much does it cost?

Every service has a visible starting price — most projects start well under ₹15,000. You'll see the exact cost in your proposal before anything begins, and it doesn't change unless you change the scope. See pricing.

How long will my project take?

A Google Business Profile takes 2–3 days. A landing page takes about a week. A full website takes 2–3 weeks. Your proposal states the exact timeline, and we tell you immediately if anything shifts.

How do payments work?

Simple: a part upfront to start, the balance at launch. Monthly services are billed month to month — no lock-in contracts. No hidden fees, ever.

Who owns the work?

You do. Your domain, your website, your designs, your accounts — all registered in your name with logins handed over at launch. If we ever part ways, everything stays with you.

What happens after launch?

Every project includes a support window after launch for fixes and questions. If you want ongoing updates, backups and monitoring, our maintenance plan starts at ₹1,499/month.

What about revisions?

Your proposal includes revision rounds — enough to get it right, clearly stated up front. We'd rather fix something now than have you live with it for years.
