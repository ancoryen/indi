The honest comparison

Four other ways to do this. Here's what each actually gets you.

You can build it yourself, buy it as gigs, hire a traditional agency, or stitch together a print shop and a freelancer. All four work. This page is about what each route costs you beyond the invoice — compared on coherence and ownership, because comparing on price flatters whoever hides the most.

Compared by model, deliberately — every category above contains good companies, and a price screenshot would be stale in a month. What doesn't go stale is structure: who does the work, who owns it, and who answers when it matters.

The ownership rule

Yours outright — once delivered and paid.

Everything we produce transfers to you in full when we confirm delivery and payment is complete: domain, site, designs, accounts, every login. Until that moment the work-in-progress is ours — which is exactly what makes the handover clean, complete and final. The fine print says the same thing the big print does: read the terms.

  • Delivered means confirmed against your proposal, in writing.
  • Paid means paid in full — then transfer is immediate.
  • After transfer, nothing is held back and nothing expires.
  • Part ways later? Everything still stays with you.
